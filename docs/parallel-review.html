What changed in the earlier reports?

The TTC generator inserted fixed purported holdout and confidence-interval numbers without running that holdout. Those numbers are withdrawn. The other branches reported private-evaluator results that this audit did not independently reproduce; the SCU leaderboard projection is not defensibly calibrated. Previous TTC, SCU, HVFD and XGHF promotion or uniqueness recommendations are withdrawn.

The legacy generators and site/card rebuilders now stop rather than regenerate unsupported approvals. Their code and earlier reports remain available in Git history for research.

Saved-byte format checks

The three original TTC, ALEDGE and SCU zero-outside files are single-band float32, match the owner-mirror grid, and have finite values in [0,1]; their unmasked outside footprint fails the conservative local policy. TTC and SCU NaN twins satisfy the outside-null check but fail the whole-array-finite check. This is not an organizer acceptance decision, and it does not diagnose the earlier portal error.
