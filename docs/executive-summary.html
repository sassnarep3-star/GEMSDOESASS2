Evidence-bounded explanation

Why H33-B2 may have improved the reported ratio

The pinned bytes establish a narrow fact: B2 is exactly D2.8 with 6,436 dots removed. Using the public catalogue proxy, 3,891 removed dots lie ≤1 pixel from known faults and 2,545 lie at >1 to ≤2 pixels; none lie farther.

Under the local DTI form, near-catalogue flank dots can add false-positive mass without adding independent hidden-fault credit. Removing them is therefore consistent with a higher ratio. It is not proof of causation because the organizer's hidden labels, components, and receipt are unavailable.

What this does not prove

It does not prove score manipulation, a defect in the competition metric, B2's actual upload identity, or that a similar pruning scheme would score well. It only explains an observable raster relationship.
