The steps to submit (only after the owner rules)

  1. Owner records a ruling on the lattice rule in IRREGULARITIES.md. Without a ruling, do not upload.
  2. Check the file hash against the SHA-256 above. Upload the zeros .tif, or the .zip with exactly one TIFF.
  3. Open the DrivenData "New submission" form (competition page). Choose the file, enter the name and note above, then submit.
  4. Record the portal receipt (score, time, file name). Only that receipt can be called ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED.
  5. Limits: the official rules allow up to three submissions per week for feedback. Only one final submission counts. Check the current allowance on the competition page before uploading.
