Decision: do not download or resubmit these historical files. They already represent prior submissions, and their strong inter-site overlap means they are not novel alternatives. GEMSDOE32 is the best reported focus-site file, not a validated predictor of the next leaderboard result.

Why GEMSDOE32 ranks first—and what that does not prove

Saved-byte forensics describe this focus family as sparse, approximately space-filling binary masks whose dot placement changes with exclusion distance from mapped faults. Among the supplied claimed values, GEMSDOE32 is highest and has fewer predicted cells than the higher-contamination GEMSDOE25/30 variants. That pattern is compatible with fewer false positives against an “off-catalogue” target, but it does not establish a geological mechanism or causal explanation for the 0.2778 result.

The official problem describes the initial hidden test as expert-labelled faults absent from the public USGS fault database and the final round as rescored against an expanded expert-reviewed truth set. A holdout against already mapped traces is therefore not a direct surrogate for the leaderboard. The official distance-weighted Tversky metric uses α=0.2, β=0.8 and a 300 m triangular kernel; a sparse map can trade false positives against missed-fault recall, but we cannot infer the optimum without the hidden truth or a calibrated external validation instrument.

Score answer: 0.2778 is the highest focus-site value reported in the user’s ledger; it is not organizer-confirmed here. A score above 0.2778 is not something this repo can currently promise or estimate reliably. The repo's earlier 0.29 “MODEL ESTIMATE” is withdrawn as unsupported extrapolation.

Submission guidance

Do not use any old one-click download until a new candidate passes (1) upstream-validated spatial holdout and leakage canary, (2) pre-placement surface and final-dot uniqueness against the complete registry with a resolved lattice rule, (3) strict format checks against an authenticated submission sample, and (4) the separate weekly-slot selector. No slot has been used by this review.
