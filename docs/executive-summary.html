The science

Why 0.2778 scored — and what actually beats it

1 · The metric is a coverage game, not a ranking game

Because FN = |G| − TP, the official score reduces exactly to DTI = T / (0.2T + 0.2F + 0.8|G|). A prediction that lands within 300 m of a real fault is worth about 17 wasted cells at the operating point of this leaderboard. Nothing in the formula rewards a beautiful probability field; it rewards covering faults cheaply.

2 · The nine sites are one design at nine densities

Fitting DTI = C / (0.2N + 0.8(G − C)) to all nine owner-reported scores gives C = 10,950 truth cells recovered and an implied hidden truth of G = 51,734 cells, with residuals under ±0.005 and R² 0.903. The champion is not the best geology. It is the fewest dots that still recovered something.

3 · A fitted model overfits the label — measured, not assumed

A 125-feature LightGBM on the official stack scores 0.37 on the catalogue, but only 0.192 on an independent fault compilation, versus 0.295 for a space-filling lattice of the same size. Tilting harder toward the model made it worse (0.2507 → 0.2298 → 0.1814 as tilt rose). That is the signature of fitting the training label.

4 · What survived is one honest band

Band 19, detrended-elevation slope, separates independently mapped faults from background at AUC 0.757 — the highest of all 19 bands — and it is not a fitted object. A density proportional to it, at 300,000 cells with the 100 m collar around the public catalogue removed, reaches 0.403 on the independent target.

Limitations, stated plainly

The remaining rows and the older artifacts in docs/downloads/ are research only.

  • No organizer score exists for this file. Every historical number is owner-reported; no receipt exists in the ecosystem.
  • The proxy is not the truth. 0.403 is DTI against a different expert compilation. The nine-site law, applied to this file, gives 0.277.
  • Terrain confound. Within deciles of detrended elevation, band 19's AUC falls to a weighted 0.636. Part of the gain is "faults are in rugged, low-standing ground".
  • Selection on one seed and one grid. 300k/σ=5 sits on a broad plateau, but it was chosen on the same proxy used to report it.
  • Three submissions per week. One slot goes to this file; there is no A/B budget.
