Decision: DO NOT SUBMIT any of these files under the unchanged literal parallel-run protocol. Another session merged three candidate families into main while this audit was underway. We did not generate, train, rescore or submit them. We reopened their exact zero-filled TIFF bytes from immutable commit 4a88f375, then compared their positive pixels against the registered witnesses using the unchanged shared-template geometry. The evidence and hashes are in evidence/concurrent-main-audit.json.

The strict stop is greater than 70% of candidate dots within three pixels of any registered raster. The lattice is a genuine dot pattern. The separate dense-surface result is conditional on the shared template's literal finite-positive support definition; whether a continuous raster should count as “dots” is a protocol question, not an exemption we can invent. Pairwise comparison of only these three or the nine highlighted sites does not satisfy the all-registry rule. Neither their surfaces nor a complete current registry were independently checked in this audit; the positive witness suffices to stop, not to certify the remaining comparisons.

All three zero-filled files reopened as single-band float32 on the owner-mirrored sample grid and their stored values were finite in [0,1]. The zero-filled outside-footprint cells are not marked null. These are local format observations, not organizer acceptance and not a diagnosis of the previous range error. Their NaN-outside variants were not rechecked here.

Why the earlier green release claims are not sufficient

  • The SCU1 prior card reports a maximum overlap against its 12 listed focus comparisons, not the registered lattice and dense witness above. Its “unique” and “OK to submit” wording is therefore too broad. The related registry manifest does not establish a complete current registry.
  • The prior SCU1 card reports OWNER-REPORTED HOLDOUT-DTI on evaluator GEMSDOESASS2-HRv1, 60,289 withheld positives: visible-only mean 0.01632, stated 95% interval [0.01076, 0.02271], versus a fixed submitted champion mean 0.00473, stated interval [0.00401, 0.00553]. We did not rerun these numbers. The code rebuilds the candidate from visible faults but keeps the champion fixed, and the evaluator averages per-fold DTI values and interval endpoints. This is not the requested pooled-component, paired shared-template comparison. These intervals are prior author claims, not independently validated CIs or evidence of a leaderboard improvement.
  • The TTC-A1 card describes its cited holdout as simulated. It cannot be treated as an actual holdout result or organizer receipt.
  • The ASS2-ALEDGE card itself records a negative decision and a paired proxy interval crossing zero, and explicitly acknowledges that its comparison was limited to the focus champions. Our lattice recheck supplies an independent stop as well.

The prior work, byte files, methods and records remain in Git history and the repository; this audit corrects the present release status, not the past experimental record. It does not establish that a future, scientifically validated candidate is impossible if the ambiguous registry protocol is explicitly resolved.
