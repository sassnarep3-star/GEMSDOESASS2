Evidence review · 2026-10-31

Why did GEMSDOE32 report 0.2778?

We cannot prove why from the repository. The value is USER-REPORTED / UNVERIFIED, with no organizer receipt tying it to the pinned raster. Saved-byte evidence supports a plausible explanation, not a causal or predictive claim.

What is measured

  • The pinned GEMSDOE32 mask has 37,654 positive cells; the user-supplied ledger reports 0.2778.
  • The local audit reports the GEMSDOE32 positive mask exactly equals GEMSDOE25 after pruning 6,436 cells within 2 pixels of the bridge catalogue.
  • GEMSDOE31 is another closely related catalogue-distance-pruned variant. GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 are pixel-identical in the saved pairwise receipt.
  • All these score labels remain USER-REPORTED / UNVERIFIED; the raster and score are not authenticated as a pair.

Detailed artifacts: pinned audit · pairwise matrix · full written autopsy.

What is plausible

The official challenge targets expert-identified faults absent from the existing public USGS database. Under the official distance-weighted Tversky metric (α=0.2, β=0.8, triangular kernel radius 300 m), dropping a prediction can help if it had little chance of receiving hidden-fault credit and otherwise increased false-positive cost. Thus, within a shared sparse-mask family, a stronger mapped-fault collar could plausibly improve the reported score.

That is a hypothesis, not a proof. New faults may lie near mapped splays or stepovers; the map may be misaligned; and the distance-weighted metric is non-additive at the individual-dot level. No “guaranteed false positive” or per-cell score contribution can be inferred without hidden truth and a validated implementation.

Bottom line: the best explanation supported by saved bytes is that GEMSDOE32 was the sparsest/most pruned member among closely related variants. This association does not establish that the collar caused 0.2778, that the file is geologically superior, or that a future file can beat it.

Why no score estimate or new submission?

  • Competition data are login-gated here and no organizer receipt exists.
  • The intended holdout is against mapped faults, while the competition's initial hidden labels are newly expert-identified faults. Without independent calibration, a mapped-fault HOLDOUT-DTI is not a leaderboard score or a valid projection.
  • The shared template evaluator is fail-closed after synthetic probes expose duplicate-detection and false-positive-accounting defects. The task says to fix that shared tool upstream, not substitute a private fork.
  • The literal uniqueness rule fails against the pinned dense r13 calibration raster. The rule gives no exception for calibration assets; none is assumed.
  • Therefore the estimated leaderboard score is NOT ESTIMABLE, no new TIF was generated, and no file is cleared to submit.
