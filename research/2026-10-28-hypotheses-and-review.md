## Why the reported GEMSDOE32 result may be high

The [pinned raster audit](../docs/data/audit.json) finds that GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have identical positive pixel masks, and the GEMSDOE32 B2 mask is exactly the GEMSDOE25 mask after removal of pixels within two grid cells of the mirrored catalogue. GEMSDOE31 corresponds to the one-cell prune. The reported 0.2778 and related scores are **USER-REPORTED / UNVERIFIED**: no organizer receipt tying them to these bytes is available.

The [official metric](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/#performance-metric) credits maximum nearby prediction strength within a 300 m triangular kernel and penalizes prediction mass away from truth; redundant dense dots can therefore increase false-positive mass without equivalent extra coverage. [DrivenData staff](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/2) say known-fault pixels are excluded from both rounds. Removing adjacent predictions **could** reduce false positives, but can also remove genuinely unmapped nearby strands. We cannot calculate the private TP/FP/FN terms or establish why the reported score changed, much less guarantee beating it.
