## Why Prior GEMSDOE Runs Plateaued at `0.2778` (Root-Cause Gap Analysis)

Forensic auditing across all 9 focus repositories (`GEMSDOE24`..`GEMSDOE36`) plus the broader GEMS ledger confirms two structural failure modes in prior submissions:

1. **Catalogue-Halo Overfitting vs. Zero-Context Blind Discovery:** In the local `buffered_component_draw` holdout (`gems57-pooled-hide-v2`, `buffer_px=3`, `boundary_px=12`), **54.51%** of withheld pixels lie within `<= 10 px` (`1 km`) of remaining visible faults and only **16.29%** lie beyond `20 px` (`2 km`). Models heavily weighted toward catalogue distance (`PTCF: 0.2343`, `H70: 0.2339`) win on buffered holdout by clustering dots in a 300–1000 m halo around existing fault tips, yet drop `0.04+` DTI on the live DrivenData test set where NLR/USGS geologists compiled whole unmapped fault corridors across newly flown GeoDAWN + 1 m 3DEP blocks. Conversely, pure ridge-cliff models (`P8: 0.2478`, `ORTHO: 0.2376`) and basin-playa geophysical models (`H76_3: 0.1555`) waste false-positive mass (`\alpha = 0.2` penalty) on non-tectonic mountain crests or thick alluvial basin centers.
2. **Untapped Hydrothermal Upflow Physics:** Blind geothermal systems in the northwestern Great Basin (~75% of regional resources lack mapped surface scarps; Faulds & Hinz 2015; Ayling et al. 2022) still leak heat, silica/carbonate fluids, and argillic/potassic alteration vertically along permeable damage zones. Prior top GEMSDOE runs (`GEMSDOE32 = 0.2778`) used zero GDR 2 m shallow temperature probes, zero paleo-geothermal sinter/travertine vents, zero spring geothermometers, and zero upward-continued magnetic magnetite-destruction troughs.

To surpass `0.2778` (`GEMSDOE32`) and `0.3195` toward the verified live leaderboard frontier (`0.4413` prompt benchmark; `0.4544` live 2026-10-27 #1 on [DrivenData Leaderboard](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/leaderboard/)), we pre-register four untried geological hypotheses ranked by expected DTI gain and implementation cost.

## Detailed Physical Formulations & Prior-Run Contrast

### 1. `H-SASS2-1` — Hydrothermal Vent–Fault Coupled Dual-Population Synthesis (`HVFD`)
* **Specific Layers:** Full 36-channel leak-free physical/hydrothermal stack (`STATIC_FEATURE_NAMES`) coupled with 6 fold-proper cross-component mechanical transfer features (`MECH_FEATURE_NAMES`: exact binary bit-plane cross-component distance `m01_xcomp_dist`, degree-1 skeleton strike-propagation lobe `m02_tip_strike_lobe`, off-axis dilational wing-crack lobe `m03_wingcrack_dilational`, opposite-parity en-echelon relay bridge `m04_relay_stepover_bridge`, leave-self-out structural belt density `m05_regional_fault_density`, and backward hydrothermal orphan-conduit boost `m06_hydro_orphan_conduit_boost`).
* **Physical Signature / Transform:** Separates fault discovery into two physically distinct populations trained via **Leave-One-Quadrant-Out (LOQO)** spatial cross-validation (`8 km` dead-band):
  1. **Expert A (`Zero-Catalogue Blind Corridor Detector`):** Trained strictly on 36 multi-sensor geomorphic, hydrothermal, potential-field, and geodetic channels (`0` catalogue inputs) so its weights cannot collapse onto mapped fault proximity.
  2. **Expert B (`Cross-Component Fracture Mechanics & Orphan-Vent Bridge`):** Trained with exact binary bit-plane leave-self-component-out features ($V \setminus C_k$ on $d_{\text{self}} \le 3\text{ px}$), eliminating the `d_vis = 0` training degeneracy that broke prior tip-halo models while linking unmapped step-overs, horsetail splays, and orphan thermal springs back to regional stress corridors.
  Fuses `Expert A` and `Expert B` with a 900 m oriented line-integral strike-collinearity filter and allocates discrete predictions via exact 300 m submodular greedy CELF maximization outside a `B = 2.0 px` zero-credit catalogue collar.
* **Why It Detects Missing USGS/INGENIOUS Faults:** Recovers both unmapped along-strike extensions/relay ramps of known Quaternary faults *and* whole unmapped range-front/intrabasin hydrothermal corridors in newly flown GeoDAWN + 1 m 3DEP blocks where NLR/USGS geologists compiled Phase 1 labels.
* **Difference from Prior Repo Runs:** Every focus-site winner (`GEMSDOE24`..`36`, `0.2449–0.2778`) is an `86.7%–100%` 3-px dot-overlap clone of `H19-5` (`h33` threshold/spacing post-processing of an older ridge baseline with zero hydrothermal vent physics and zero cross-component fracture mechanics). `HVFD` trains from scratch on a clean 42-channel physical + cross-component stack and enforces `< 70%` 3-px overlap and `<= 0.90` Spearman rank correlation against all 576 registry/focus/scored `.tif` files.
* **Named Non-Fault Process Mimic:** Convex erosional mountain ridge crests and highway/railroad cut-and-fill embankments (explicitly suppressed via `f04_ridge_crest_convexity_deconf` and `f13_road_safe_weight`).

### 2. `H-SASS2-2` — Hydrothermal Magnetite-Destruction & K-Metasomatism Conduit (`HMDV`)
* **Specific Layers:** Competition `rtp` (Band 2), `iso_grav_anom_hg` (Band 18), `cond_surf` (Band 17); USGS GeoDAWN (`10.5066/P93LGLVQ`, CC0) `geodawn_rad_u8.tif` (`K`, `Th`) and `geodawn_extensions_u8.tif` (`U/Th`, 150 m upward-continued `tmi_up150`); DOE GDR INGENIOUS (`10.15121/1881483`, CC-BY-4.0) `gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv` (`temp_c`, `geothermquartz_c`, `geothermchalc_c`, `geothermcat_c` — **explicitly stripping `dist_known_fault_px`**), `derived_gdr_2m_probes_100m_u8.tif` (`f2mdab`), and `derived_gdr_paleo_100m_u8.tif` (holocene/pleistocene sinter, travertine & tufa mounds).
* **Physical Signature / Transform:** Oxidizing/acid-sulfate hydrothermal fluids ascending along active permeable faults leach primary titanomagnetite into non-magnetic pyrite/hematite/illite-smectite while precipitating adularia/illite ($K$ enrichment over immobile $Th$) and smectite clay caps (high shallow MT `cond_surf` gradient). We compute the shallow demagnetization trough $\max(z(\text{TMI}_{\text{up150}}) - z(\text{RTP}_{\text{HP}}),\, 0)\sqrt{\max(z(|\nabla g_{\text{iso}}|), 0) + 0.15}$ coupled with anisotropic N18°E/N38°W conduit kernels seeded at thermal wells/springs, 2 m temperature probes, and paleo-sinter mounds.
* **Why It Detects Missing USGS/INGENIOUS Faults:** Blind and incipient range-front/accommodation-zone faults buried beneath thin late-Pleistocene fans or playas lack classic multi-meter topographic scarps in 1970s–2000s USGS quadrangle mapping, but stand out sharply in 2022–2024 GeoDAWN high-resolution magnetic/radiometric flights and INGENIOUS thermal compilations.
* **Difference from Prior Repo Runs:** Prior GEMSDOE focus sites did not couple upward-continued magnetic attenuation troughs with 2 m temperature probes, chemical geothermometers, and paleo-sinter vents.
* **Named Non-Fault Process Mimic:** Felsic volcanic ash-flow tuffs (naturally K-rich and weakly magnetic) and evaporitic playa brines (electrically conductive without fault permeability), gated out by requiring coincident lateral density contrast (`iso_grav_anom_hg`) and piedmont/basement gradient structure.

### 3. `H-SASS2-3` — Piedmont Break-in-Slope Polarity & Fluvial $\chi$-Knickzone De-confounding (`PBSC`)
* **Specific Layers:** Competition `det_elev` (Band 12), `det_elev_slope` (Band 19); USGS 3DEP 10 m (`topo_10m_derived_100m_u8.tif`: sub-pixel `slope_std`, `curv_prof`, `aspect_coh × hs_lineament`); 10 m fluvial $\chi$-profile disequilibrium (`dem10_chi_100m_i16.tif`: `step_max_x1000`, `ksn_x100`); USGS 1 m 3DEP LiDAR scarp stack (`lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif`: `scarp_exceed`, `step_height`, `down_slope`, `up_slope`, `orient_coh`); US Census TIGER/Line (`tiger_road_distance_m.tif`, CC0).
* **Physical Signature / Transform:** Active Basin-and-Range normal faults rupture at the **piedmont break-in-slope** (`det_elev_slope` $\in [2^\circ, 9^\circ]$, peak $|\nabla \text{det\_elev\_slope}|$) with one-sided elevation-step polarity (`|down_slope - up_slope| / (down_slope + up_slope + c)`), generating transient upstream fluvial steepness ($\chi$-step knickzones) across range-front drainages. By simultaneously feeding convex crest curvature $-\nabla^2(\text{det\_elev})$ and road-embankment proximity (`tiger_road_distance_m`), the classifier separates true tectonic fault-line scarps from symmetric erosional spurs and highway grades.
* **Why It Detects Missing USGS/INGENIOUS Faults:** Captures subtle 0.5–2 m degraded alluvial-fan scarps and bedrock-piedmont fault-line junctions newly visible in 3DEP 1 m / 10 m DEMs that were unmapped prior to NLR/USGS's 2025–2026 GEMS compilation.
* **Difference from Prior Repo Runs:** Replaces raw `det_elev_slope` thresholding (which caused `P8`/`ORTHO` to dump 70%+ of dots on high mountain ridges) with explicit piedmont inflection + $\chi$-knickzone + crest/road de-confounding.
* **Named Non-Fault Process Mimic:** Pleistocene pluvial Lake Lahontan wave-cut shorelines and highway cut-and-fill embankments (`I-80` / `US-50` / `US-95`).

### 4. `H-SASS2-4` — Sentinel-1 OPERA Vertical Subsidence Differential (`OPERA-DISP`, Deferred Reserve)
* **Specific Layers:** NASA JPL / ASF DAAC OPERA Level-3 DISP-S1 surface displacement velocity (`CC0` U.S. Government Public Domain, [ASF OPERA Documentation](https://asf.alaska.edu/datasets/daac/opera/)) paired with competition `depth_to_base` (Band 15) and `geod_dilatation_rate` (Band 8).
* **Physical Signature / Transform:** Sharp horizontal phase-velocity gradients $|\nabla v_{\text{LOS}}|$ where differential aquifer compaction or geothermal reservoir pressure change terminates abruptly against an impermeable fault-core gouge seal.
* **Status & Cost Trade-Off:** Ranked #4 due to high download/mosaic cost (dozens of multi-GB OPERA bursts + Earthdata token handling inside sandbox egress limits) and severe confounding from agricultural groundwater pumping bowls in Fallon, Mason Valley, and Lovelock.
