02 / What the evidence says

A strong result, with limits.

Separate a useful scientific explanation from a score claim we cannot verify.

Why the reported 0.2778 may lead

Less redundant, not necessarily more certain.

The pinned H33-B2 support is a subset of the B1 file, with 2,545 fewer positive pixels. The GEMSDOE36 historical file contains B2 plus 1,200 more.

Under the official distance-weighted metric, thinning can reduce false-positive mass while preserving nearby coverage. Hidden labels and score receipts are unavailable, so this is a plausible mechanism—not a proven cause or a score forecast.

03 / Research direction

Curious, but not careless.

Ranked ideas are hypotheses—not assigned lanes, measured improvements, or submission recommendations.

01 / FIRST TO TEST

Displaced magnetic texture

Test multi-scale, signed cross-strike texture shifts against gravity contrast. Unfaulted lithologic contacts can mimic the signature.

02 / DATA BLOCKED

Compositional contrasts

Compare radiometric flank ratios around magnetic edges, with cover and survey bias controls. File-level coverage is not verified.

03 / DATA BLOCKED

Repeated terrain steps

Look for aligned topographic profile breaks. Shorelines, alluvial terraces and engineered cuts are serious alternatives.
