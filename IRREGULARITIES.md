# Irregularities & Leakage Audit Ledger — `GEMSDOESASS2`

Every data anomaly, target-leakage hazard, evaluation domain shift, and cross-repository duplicate uncovered during autonomous forensic verification is documented below with exact file paths, measurements, and active mitigations.

| ID | Category | Finding (Exact Measurement) | Impact on Competition | Active Mitigation in `GEMSDOESASS2` |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`IR-SASS2-01`** | **Target Leakage Hazard** | `external/gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv` contains precomputed column `dist_known_fault_px` (`21,164` rows) measuring Euclidean distance to the full `existing_faults.tif` (`labels.tif`) raster. | Using `dist_known_fault_px` leaks withheld fault locations into training/holdout evaluation (`Rule 2 & Rule 4` violation). | Explicitly ignored in `src/gems_sass2/features.py` (`dist_known_fault_px_stripped: True` verified by unit test `tests/test_leakage_and_submission.py`). |
| **`IR-SASS2-02`** | **Target Leakage Hazard** | Historical `sb_slip_tendency_in_footprint.csv` (USGS ScienceBase INGENIOUS slip/dilation tendency lines) is computed directly along mapped INGENIOUS quaternary fault vertices (`84.6%` within `<= 300 m` of `labels.tif`). | Rasterizing slip-tendency polylines leaks withheld fault segments (`single-feature AUC > 0.90`). | Excluded completely from `registry/data_manifest.json` and `src/gems_sass2/features.py`. |
| **`IR-SASS2-03`** | **9-Site Duplicate Lineage** | Forensic audit of all 9 focus repositories (`GEMSDOE24`, `25`, `27`, `28`, `30`, `31`, `32`, `33`, `36`) proves every site's highest-scoring submission (`0.2449` to `0.2778`) shares **`97.8%` to `100.0%` 3-px dot overlap** (`86.7%–100.0%` vs `GEMSDOE32 B2`) with ancestor `H19-5` (`h19_5_scored.tif`). Furthermore, `GEMSDOE25` (`d28-nan`) and `GEMSDOE30` (`d28-zeros`) are exact in-footprint pixel-value clones (`0.2600`) differing only in outside-footprint `NaN` vs `0.0`. | Re-submitting any `h19_5`/`h33` post-processing variant violates the Parallel-Run Protocol (`<= 70%` 3-px overlap, `|Spearman| <= 0.90`) and wastes `3/week` submission slots ([DOE Official Rules Sec. 3.2](https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy26osti/96647.pdf)). | `HVFD` trains from scratch with zero prior submission inputs and enforces pre-placement Spearman (`<= 0.90`) and post-placement 3-px overlap (`<= 0.70`) gates across all **`451` unique (`542` scanned)** `.tif` files in the focus & historical ledger (`max_3px = 68.27%`). |
| **`IR-SASS2-04`** | **Training-to-Inference Distance Collapse (`d_vis = 0`)** | On any fold, positive training pixels lie *on* `visible` faults (`d_vis = 0 px`), whereas every off-catalogue evaluation pixel has `d_vis >= 1 px` (and in `buffered_component_draw`, `d_vis > 3 px`). Naive classifiers trained with raw `EDT(visible)` assign degenerate weight to `d_vis == 0` and collapse at inference. | Causes severe miscalibration of mechanical features (`PTCF: 0.2343`, `H70: 0.2339`). | Implemented exact binary bit-plane **leave-self-component-out** distance (`_exact_cross_component_distance` in `src/gems_sass2/features.py`) and parity-split tip/relay fields so training positives on $C_k$ see $V \setminus C_k$ (`d > 3 px`), matching withheld components. |
| **`IR-SASS2-05`** | **Holdout vs. Live Domain Shift** | In `gems57-pooled-hide-v2` (`buffered_component_draw`, `K=11,321`), **`54.51%`** of withheld fault pixels lie within `<= 10 px` (`1 km`) of remaining visible faults and only **`16.29%`** lie at `> 20 px`. In contrast, live winner `GEMSDOE32` (`0.2778`) places **`49.2%`** of dots at `> 20 px` (`54.4%` piedmont, `45.1%` mountain, `0.5%` basin). | Optimizing solely on `buffered_component_draw` over-rewards tight 300–800 m catalogue halos and penalizes blind corridor discovery. | Every candidate is scored on **both** `gems57-pooled-hide-v2` (`buffered_component_draw`, `K=11,321`) and zero-context blind spatial quadrants (`spatial.folds`, `buffer_px=80`, `K=60,988`), and `Expert A` is trained with **zero** catalogue features. |
| **`IR-SASS2-06`** | **DrivenData Portal `[0, 1]` / `NaN` Rejection Trap** | DrivenData's automated validator rejects rasters containing `NaN` inside the footprint, values `< 0` or `> 1`, wrong band count, or ambiguous `nodata` handling (`"Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]"`). Note also `training_features.tif` uses `-3.4028235e+38` sentinels in partial-coverage bands (`cond_surf` missing `15.4%` of footprint; `lidar_scarp` missing `51.8%` of footprint). | Uncleaned sentinels propagate `NaN`/`Inf` into model predictions and trigger portal rejection. | All feature bands pass through `_clean_band` + finite `robust_z`; final deliverable is emitted via `vendor/gems57/submission_writer.py` (`-zeros.tif`, `nodata=None`, `0.0` outside footprint, all `12,279,160` pixels finite `float32` in `[0.0, 1.0]`, `0` positives on `existing_faults.tif`) and verified by `vendor/gems57/validate.py`. |
| **`IR-SASS2-07`** | **Live Leaderboard Progression Beyond Prompt Snapshot** | Prompt cites `0.4413` as leaderboard top at prompt drafting time; live verification of [DrivenData Leaderboard](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/leaderboard/) on `2026-10-27` shows `#1 xiaofanhu = 0.4544`, `#2 cfld__ = 0.3916`, `#3 Batik Shirt Brothers = 0.3911`, `#4 BrandenKMurray = 0.3724`, `#5 No Fault of Our Own = 0.3566`. | Stale benchmark targets can misstate Phase 1 top-5 (`$10,000` each) cutoff (`0.3566`). | Both the prompt's `0.4413` reference and the live `2026-10-27` verified `0.4544` high (and `0.3566` top-5 prize cutoff) are transparently reported with timestamps. |
| **`IR-SASS2-08`** | **Post-Masked `B=2` Submission `.tif` Holdout Handicap** | Scoring an already-exported full-catalogue `B=2` submission `.tif` retrospectively against `gems57-pooled-hide-v2` yields artificially depressed scores (`0.0028–0.0289`) because the full-catalogue `d_cat <= 2.0 px` collar already zeroed all `0–200 m` pixels around withheld truth! | Confusing post-masked raster scores with fold-proper OOF holdout scores invalidates cross-site comparisons. | Our 9-Site Audit Table explicitly reports **both** each repo's fold-proper OOF Run Card `HOLDOUT-DTI` (masked only against `visible`) and post-masked raster diagnostics. |
| **`IR-SASS2-09`** | **Sibling Repo State Mismatches (`GEMSDOE30`, `GEMSDOE28`, `GEMSDOE36`)** | `GEMSDOE30`'s latest `.tif` lacks a Run Card and contains `7,111,787` outside-footprint `NaN`s; `GEMSDOE28`'s `s22c` (`0.1202`) used an **unbuffered** quadrant split (`buffered: false`); `GEMSDOE36`'s `README.md` promotes `KPL-36 EXP-3` (`0.1701` on `N=60,988`) while `docs/downloads/run_card.json` and `docs/index.html` show a prior `negative` decision. | Unchecked sibling repos risk portal NaN errors or submitting unbuffered/superseded candidates. | Flagged individually in `evidence/nine_sites_audit.json` and `docs/executive-summary.html`; `GEMSDOESASS2` (`0.11842` buffered LOQO; `0.17630` on `N=60,988` blind-8km; `0` NaN; `61.6%` overlap vs `KPL-36`) supersedes all 9 sites as Primary Slot #1. |
