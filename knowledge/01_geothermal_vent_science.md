# Geothermal-fault science: sourced starting points

The competition predicts **fault structures**, not verified geothermal vents. A predicted structural line is not proof of a permeable conduit, a geothermal reservoir, or a commercially viable resource. The [DrivenData problem description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) explains that the hidden test set comprises newly expert-identified faults; the final round also includes expert-verified additions.

| Evidence | What the official source supports | Use / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| [USGS GeoDAWN](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and) | Airborne magnetic and radiometric surveys of western Nevada and eastern California; companion lidar, multiple survey blocks, varying flight height, flight-line and tie-line details. Release marked CC0. | Magnetic boundaries and radiometric contrast may help characterize geology. Lithologic contacts, terrain clearance and acquisition/leveling artifacts can mimic a fault. Surface radiometrics alone cannot prove hydrothermal alteration. |
| [GDR 1391 — INGENIOUS compilation](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391) | Public CC BY 4.0 regional compilation lists 2-m temperature surveys, spring/well temperature and chemistry, paleo-geothermal deposits, geodesy, gravity and other data. | Potential geothermal evidence and counterfactuals. Sampling is uneven and likely biased toward known prospects; metadata, spatial coverage, permissions and transformations need checking. A fault-derived column must not leak hidden folds. |
| [USGS blind geothermal play-fairway work](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/discovering-blind-geothermal-systems-great-basin-region-integrated-geologic-and) | Integrates geological and geophysical evidence for blind geothermal systems. | Supports a multidisciplinary hypothesis, not a quantified probability or leaderboard gain for this raster. |
| [USGS 3DEP](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program) | Elevation datasets support topographic interpretation. | Erosion, shorelines, roads and scarps from other processes can resemble faulting. Specific tiles and coverage have not been revalidated here. |

Candidate interactions and named non-fault mimics are in [the current preregistration](../research/2026-10-28-hypotheses-and-review.md). They have not been tested in this session. Earlier numerical claims about thermal halo size, alteration polarity, percentages of resources and guaranteed physical diagnostics were not established by the sources above and have been withdrawn; see Git history for the previous text.
