### 1.2 Exact Distance-Weighted Tversky (`DW-Tversky`) Mechanism
From the official DrivenData Problem Description ([page/967](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/)) and organizer confirmation ([staff post #11516](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516)), known catalogue pixels $C$ (`labels.tif`, $60,988$ pixels) are excluded from the evaluation domain $E = F \setminus C$, and binary predictions $P \subset E$ are scored against unmapped private faults $G \subset E$ with $R = 300\text{ m} = 3\text{ px}$, $K(u) = \max(0, 1 - u/3)$, $\alpha = 0.2$, $\beta = 0.8$:

$$\text{DTI}(P, G) = \frac{TP_w}{TP_w + 0.2\,FP_w + 0.8\,FN_w}, \qquad TP_w = \sum_{g \in G} K(d(g, P)), \qquad FP_w = \sum_{p \in P} \bigl(1 - K(d(p, G))\bigr), \qquad FN_w = |G| - TP_w.$$

Three structural consequences immediately explain the `0.1922 → 0.2477 → 0.2600 → 0.2708 → 0.2778` climb:
1. **Why binary sub-kernel crests beat continuous bands (`0.1922 → 0.2600`):** Because $TP_w$ takes a **pointwise supremum** $\max_{p \in P} K(d(g, p))$ over the $3\text{ px}$ cone whereas $FP_w$ **sums linearly** over every predicted pixel, a 3-pixel-wide continuous ridge pays $3\times$ the false-positive mass for almost identical $TP_w$. Spacing dots at $d \approx 2.55\text{–}2.80\text{ px}$ along fault strike preserves $\ge 53\text{–}100\%$ kernel credit along a continuous trace while cutting $FP_w$ by $>63\%$.
2. **Why `B=2` (`>200 m` off `labels.tif`) beat `B=0` and `B=1` (`0.2600 → 0.2708 → 0.2778`):** `labels.tif` is rasterized at $100\text{ m}$, whereas mapping positional uncertainty between USGS QFaults, 1 m LiDAR scarps, and 24k geologic quads is $\pm 100\text{–}250\text{ m}$. Predictions placed at $1\text{–}2\text{ px}$ parallel to an already-mapped fault sit inside the mapping-jitter halo of a **known** fault rather than a genuinely new fault; because $C$ is masked out, those pixels accrue zero $TP_w$ on $C$ and pay full $0.2\,FP_w$ whenever no separate unmapped strand exists within $300\text{ m}$.
3. **Marginal-dot optimality criterion:** Adding a dot $x$ with marginal $\Delta TP_w, \Delta FP_w$ improves $\text{DTI}$ if and only if
   $$\Delta TP_w \cdot (TP_w + 0.2\,FP_w + 0.8\,FN_w) > TP_w \cdot (0.2\,\Delta TP_w + 0.2\,\Delta FP_w) \iff \frac{\Delta TP_w}{\Delta TP_w + \Delta FP_w} > \frac{0.2\,\text{DTI}}{1 + 0.8\,\text{DTI}}.$$
   At $\text{DTI} = 0.2778$, an isolated dot with $\Delta TP_w + \Delta FP_w \approx 1$ needs expected credit $> 0.0455$; at today's live #1 (`xiaofanhu = 0.4544` as of 2026-10-27; `0.4413` Oct 25; `0.3195` Oct 3), that bar rises to $> 0.0667$. Note carefully: because private test truth $G$ and organizer score receipts are private, historical `0.2449–0.2778` figures remain `OWNER-REPORTED` (not `ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED`), and exact private $|G|$ or recall cannot be inverted from the public ladder alone.
